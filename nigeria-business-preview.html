Before you act

Record a dated supplier quote, confirm the unit (bag, crate, litre or piece), ask about transport and handling, and check stock. A screenshot of a transfer is not settlement evidence: confirm receipt through your own bank records. No bank is connected here.

Show the calculation and limits

Sold units = planned units × (1 − waste%). Revenue = sold units × selling price. Batch cost = all purchased units × unit cost + transport + generator hours × hourly cost. Contribution = revenue − batch cost. Cash today = starting cash + revenue collected today − batch cost. Unpaid sales are not cash. Fractional sold units are an expected-value model; actual items may be indivisible. Rent, wages, tax and other overheads are excluded unless added to your assumptions.
