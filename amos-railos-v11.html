Never treat this app as permission to work on a live railway.

5. Customer approves a clear quote.

Record what is included, what is excluded, and who is responsible.

💡 Easy Guide — Explain It Like I'm 10

Track maintenance

Keeping train tracks in good condition so trained railway teams can use them safely.

Sleeper

The support under the rails that helps keep them in position.

Roller

A heavy machine that presses ground or material down firmly.

Dumper

A machine that moves loose material around a worksite.

Rail fastening

Parts and tools used to secure rails to their supports. The exact machine Amos described is still to be confirmed.

Quote

A written estimate of what a job may cost. It is not a final contract.

Competency

Proof that a person has the right training, experience and current permission for a particular job.
