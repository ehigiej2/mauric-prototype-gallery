# MONARCH RISE — Edo Second Chance & Start Earning Pilot
Status: REVIEW ONLY · CROWN HOLD · 2026-11-13
Owner: CROWN × GENESIS × AION / OWOW
Scope: Edo State, Nigeria; adult learners; voluntary participation.

## Mission
Rebuild • Identify Strengths • Skills • Earn. Nobody automatically excluded because of a conviction, unemployment, poverty or past hardship. Protect passengers, children, homes, money and data with role-specific controls. The academy is a route to meaningful opportunities, not a guarantee of employment or earnings.

## Participants and tracks
- Young adults / graduates: digital basics → practical portfolio → supervised customer-support, catalogue or inventory tasks.
- Motor-park workers: lawful dispatch, queues, transparent authorised fees, digital reconciliation, customer care.
- People in hardship: low-pressure, accessible, flexible learning, referrals to appropriate support.
- Former offenders: confidential, individually assessed progression; no public labels, no automatic rejection.
- People currently in custody in Edo: education and *simulated/offline* tasks only until written Nigerian Correctional Service approval for any real work.

## Distinctive features
1. Osayi voice-led discovery in Pidgin/English, with human alternatives and no requirement to disclose criminal history to peers.
2. A portable learner-owned skills portfolio; share only with informed consent.
3. Six-button workspace: My Strengths / Learn / Practise / Opportunities / My Progress / Ask for Help.
4. Demonstration-based micro-credentials, not certificates issued solely for attendance.
5. Employer tasks are broken into small, transparent, properly compensated jobs where authorised.
6. Optional pay-it-forward mentoring, with boundaries and safeguarding.
7. Accessible offline-first materials and printable exercises, with no assumption of phones or internet in custody.
8. No mandatory subscription or paid job application.

## Pilot design — proposal, not approval
- One NCoS-authorised custodial centre in Edo State; 10–15 consenting adult learners; 12 weeks.
- One suitably vetted instructor weekly, OR a fortnightly schedule with revised duration and equivalent contact time.
- Facility-approved devices/materials only. No personal smartphones, live customer data, passenger locations, production credentials, payment access or unrestricted network.
- Week 1–2: digital basics and safety; 3–4: fictional product listings; 5–6: mock stock ledger; 7–8: simulated customer service; 9–10: QA and responsible AI; 11–12: portfolio and practical assessment.
- Weekly exercises: paper or approved offline computer; marked by instructor; accommodations for literacy, disability and language.
- Correctional authority must approve participant selection, facilities, equipment, instructor access, content, storage, supervision and complaints channels.

## Formal gates
G0: Confirm NCoS federal jurisdiction, Edo facility and legal counsel; no contact or commitments under CROWN HOLD.
G1: Written facility/NCoS authorisation and safeguarding/data-protection assessment.
G2: Instructor vetting, qualifications, fair pay, approved contract, equipment and accessibility plan.
G3: Pilot training only using synthetic data; independent safeguarding oversight and incident procedures.
G4: Skills assessments, voluntary portfolio and re-entry support; consent-based sharing.
G5: Any *paid custodial work* separately requires written authority, legal review, voluntary informed consent, lawful compensation, hours, safe supervision, audit, data restrictions and an independent complaint channel.
G6: Any customer-facing role requires individual role-specific screening, legal eligibility, training and human review; alternative pathways remain available.

## Role-access matrix
- Classroom synthetic listings/inventory: suitable for supervised training; no production data.
- Post-release low-risk cataloguing or internal QA: possible after consent, skills check and ordinary worker safeguards.
- Money handling / financial services: regulated partner eligibility, enhanced role checks and segregation of duties.
- Passenger driving / courier / airport document recovery / entering homes: verified licensing where required, identity, insurance, role-specific suitability and safety checks. No automatic assignment.
- Children or vulnerable-person services: additional safeguarding and lawful vetting.
- Custodial commercial tasks: DISABLED until G5 approved.
Criminal-history information: minimum necessary, confidential, restricted access, retention limits, correction and appeal. No automated criminal-risk score.

## Safeguarding and dignity
No forced labour, no unpaid production disguised as homework, no exploitative wages, no fee to access basic learning or apply for work, no guaranteed jobs, no unsafe prison connectivity, no publishing participant criminal histories. Distinguish remand, conviction and release status. Protect victims, customers, workers and learners; provide human appeals and alternative roles.

## Twelve acceptance scenarios
1. New graduate discovers strengths and finishes mock inventory exercise.
2. Motor-park worker learns lawful dispatch and refuses unauthorised levies.
3. Learner opts out without penalty.
4. Offline homework survives interruption and reconciles without duplication.
5. Prison learner cannot access live passenger/customer data.
6. Prison learner cannot receive paid production tasks before G5.
7. Former offender receives low-risk role review without blanket exclusion.
8. Restricted passenger role is withheld pending lawful checks, with alternate work suggested.
9. Portfolio shared only on specific consent and revocable where feasible.
10. Fake job offer/payment redirection is blocked and reported.
11. Complaints and safeguarding escalation reach an authorised human.
12. No claims of verified earnings or lower reoffending without measured evidence.

## Success measures
Completion, assessed competency, voluntary job applications, verified paid placements, actual net earnings, 30/90-day retention, learner satisfaction, safety events, complaints resolution, employer satisfaction. Any reoffending evaluation requires appropriate lawful design, permissions and careful attribution.
