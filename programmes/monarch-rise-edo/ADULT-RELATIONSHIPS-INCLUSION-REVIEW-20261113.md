1. Adult relationships: consensual dating, relationship advice, communication and social events. No guaranteed matches.

3. Companionship and community: age-appropriate, lawful non-exploitative social connection; do not assume it means sexual services.
4. Nightlife/creative/hospitality: lawful skills, events, employment and business tools under normal marketplace and safeguarding checks.
5. MONARCH RISE: voluntary alternative skills and livelihoods for adults who request them, including people leaving unsafe or exploitative situations. Never require disclosure of past sexual activity, convictions or relationships.

## Safety invariants

- Affirmative mutual consent; no public contact details by default; granular visibility and block/report.
- Protect location, intimate information, identity and communications; data minimisation, retention/deletion and tenant isolation. No cross-context advertising/profiling of sensitive information.
- No stalking, coercion, extortion, romance fraud, impersonation, paid intimacy booking, trafficking facilitation or recruitment into exploitative work.
- Do not expose precise location or share sensitive data across Osayi, RISE, Waka, Trade or other services without appropriate consent, legal basis and CROWN approval.

- Qualified mental-health, safeguarding and legal professionals retain their professional responsibilities; AI cannot independently diagnose or approve.
- Verify local Nigerian and cross-border legal requirements, age assurance, data protection, safety and consumer obligations before product admission.
- No user identity inference based on visiting these routes.
