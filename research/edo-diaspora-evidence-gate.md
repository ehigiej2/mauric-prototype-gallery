# Edo Diaspora — public evidence and prototype tailoring gate

Status: DRAFT / CROWN HOLD / independent research / no official endorsement
Date assessed: 2026-11-09

## Source-backed facts

1. Edo State Government (2025-04-11) described the Edo State Diaspora Agency as a bridge between Edo people abroad and institutions at home, focusing on grassroots development, skills, resources, engagement, collaboration, investment, cultural heritage and welfare.
   Source: https://edostate.gov.ng/edo-state-govt-strengthens-diaspora-engagement-for-inclusive-development/
2. Edo State Government newsletter (2025-05-05 to 2025-05-11) reported engagement with over 200 Edo indigenes worldwide after dedicated hotline/email launch; enquiries included investment, welfare and medical-care information. Historical figure only, not a current count.
   Source: https://edostate.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/EDSG-GH-Newsletter-5th-11th-May.pdf
3. Edo Diaspora UK describes community engagement, education, cultural promotion and wellbeing services in Greater Manchester. This is a distinct organisation, not the Edo State Diaspora Agency.
   Source: https://www.edodiasporauk.org/
4. Edo National Association Worldwide publicly describes cultural, youth, education and health-related programmes. Distinct from Edo State Diaspora Agency.
   Source: https://enaworldwide.org/

## Inferences requiring owner/agency validation

- A voluntary diaspora skills-to-project matching prototype may align with published engagement objectives.
- A fictional Edo farm-to-market-to-export workflow may help test whether diaspora entrepreneurs value transparent opportunity discovery.
- Healthcare workforce and procurement research scenarios may be useful, but are not official hospital operations.
- A simple feedback cockpit may help demonstrate structured user testing; it must not be presented as a live Agency service.
