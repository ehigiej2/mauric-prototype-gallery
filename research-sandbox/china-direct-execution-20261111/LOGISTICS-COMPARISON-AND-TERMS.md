## Proposed logistics framework for later founder/legal review
Parties, legal entities, service addresses and governing law: TBD. Nonexclusive; no minimum spend or shipment guarantee. No binding effect intended from this draft.
1. Warehouse must acknowledge externally purchased goods from multiple named manufacturers, authorised receiving address, parcel matching and receipt photos/weights within agreed SLA. Reject prohibited cargo.
2. Itemise receiving, storage/free days, inspection scope, repacking, consolidation, pick-pack, air/sea, insurance, customs/levies, clearance, port/storage/demurrage and final Benin delivery. State actual vs volumetric weight divisor, CBM minima, rounding and remeasurement disputes.
3. Identify exporter, importer of record, licensed broker, carrier and subcontractors; no undocumented customs shortcut, undervaluation or false product description.
4. Agree dispatch trigger, latest consolidation cut-off, tracking milestones, delivery window, customs delay escalation and signed/photo delivery evidence; no fabricated guaranteed arrival.
5. Disclose insurer, policy number/scope, valuation, deductible, exclusions, loss/damage claim deadlines and liability caps for legal review. Warehouse receipt and insurance are not assumed equivalent.
6. No release without lawful buyer instructions. Returns, failed delivery, damage, salvage and refund responsibility itemised. No unauthorised disposal or extra charges.
7. Minimum necessary customer data, purpose limitation, tenant separation, approved subprocessors, access control, retention/deletion and breach notification. Customer ownership retained.
8. Existing licensed bank/protected platform payments by authorised buyer only; MONARCH no custody or credit. Quotes valid until written timestamp. No automatic renewal, exclusivity or undisclosed commissions.
9. Complaints and protected appeals with evidence access, cure period, independent escalation and legal-review dispute process. Termination and shipment-in-transit protection agreed before execution.
