2026-11-11 | Research only | CROWN HOLD
## Decision
Assume Nigeria-first demand test, UK comparison market. No confirmed supplier relationships, live inventory, quotations, client mandates, sales or shipments. No China trip until remote evidence establishes incremental value.
## Categories
Wave 1: refined solid-colour adult apparel and children's everyday clothing, bags. No Ankara/wax/African prints. Wave 2: non-electrical phone cases and accessories. Controlled later: genuine phones/laptops, chargers, batteries and safety-sensitive children's goods.
## Fulfilment models to compare
1. Supplier direct-to-consumer one parcel, individually priced.
2. Supplier-to-independent China warehouse for aggregation and quality inspection; batch dispatch to licensed Nigeria importer/fulfilment agent.
3. Supplier-to-client bulk only where genuine prepaid verified demand supports MOQ.
4. Local Nigerian supplier alternative as landed-cost benchmark.
Never assume Alibaba wholesale suppliers offer dropshipping. Verify order-by-order pick-pack, inventory feeds, returns and customs responsibilities.
## Supplier discovery and qualification
Search Alibaba RFQs, 1688, verified factory directories; capture supplier legal name, listing URL, item specs, MOQ, inventory and quote validity. Verify identity, genuine product provenance, certifications, sample policy, packaging dimensions, returns, warranty, bank details independently, protected payment route. Treat platform badges as signals not guarantees.

## Logistics enquiry draft — NOT SENT
'Can you receive goods bought independently from multiple Chinese suppliers, inspect and consolidate them, and quote separately warehouse, pick-pack, air/sea, insurance, customs clearance, taxes, and Benin City delivery? Provide full written exclusions, prohibited goods, responsibility for importer of record, claims and returns. No volume commitment.'
## Landed cost
goods + China inland + warehouse/pick-pack + inspection + freight + insurance + customs duty + VAT/levies + clearance + Nigeria last mile + FX + returns/risk allowance. Divide by expected accepted units; compare against local Nigeria retail and UK delivery. UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN.
## Legal and safety

Nigeria VAT generally 7.5% where applicable, exact taxable base/product exceptions verified per shipment. Confirm SONCAP/NCC/NESREA where applicable; counterfeit and unsafe goods prohibited.
