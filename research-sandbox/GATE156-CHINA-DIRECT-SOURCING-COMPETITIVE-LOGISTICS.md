## Required client brief
Item and exact technical specification; quantity and MOQ; quality/certifications; unit price and currency; source city; delivery address (Lagos/Benin or elsewhere); urgency; packed gross weight, dimensions/CBM, number of cartons; incoterms; import classification and legal restrictions; buyer ownership and budget ceiling; negotiation authority and approvals. UNKNOWN is not zero.

## Written quotation requirements
Supplier: legal identity, manufacturing/trading status, model/grade, MOQ, samples, production time, China domestic delivery, incoterms, defects and refunds, bank account independently verified.
Freight: China receiving address and acceptance policy for independently sourced goods, storage and consolidation, packing/inspection, chargeable weight/CBM basis, rate currency/FX, departure and ETA range, customs classification, duties/VAT inclusion and evidence, insurance, liability and claims, last mile Benin, prohibited goods, demurrage and storage, quotation expiry.
Compare like-for-like duty-paid/cleared to final destination; avoid double counting taxes or paying twice for shipping/inspection.

## Landed cost model
total = goods + China domestic shipping + agent fees + inspection + repacking + freight + insurance + lawful import duty + VAT + clearing/port fees + Nigeria last-mile + FX/transaction costs + expected documented loss/risk reserve.
per_usable_unit = total / verified expected usable units (if denominator >0). Where unknown, report RANGE/UNKNOWN and do not fabricate savings.
Negotiation target: price, MOQ, defects policy, shipping packaging, volume consolidation, warehouse fees, delivery lead time. No misleading authority claims.

## First research basket

2. Food-service tools and suya packaging, subject to food-contact safety rules.

4. Fashion/textiles where client specifies SKUs and sizes.
Do not procure regulated mining, hazardous, medical, animal-health or food products without specialist checks.

## Decision gates
Prepare → verify supplier and freight terms → compare 2+ like-for-like routes → independent risk review → client owner review → separate founder approval for any external action → client-approved order via existing licensed bank → shipment evidence → reconciliation.
Never act as agent without written client mandate defining negotiation limits and no authority to bind client absent approval.
Revenue: optional transparent service fee or documented net-savings share only after client agreement; no undisclosed rebates or conflicted supplier commissions.
