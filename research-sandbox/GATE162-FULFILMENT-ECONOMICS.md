## Route comparison for each exact SKU
A: China supplier -> Nigeria consumer individually, with freight, duty/VAT, tracking, returns.
B: China supplier -> independent China warehouse -> consolidated shipment -> Nigeria licensed importer/last mile; include warehouse, pick-pack, clearance and delay.
C: Existing Nigerian seller -> local buyer; verify retail/wholesale pricing, availability and returns.
Each route must compare total cost per accepted usable unit, delivery promise, consumer protections, seller/importer identity, defective-goods handling, refund and return cost, FX, payment protection and no double counting.
## Research gates
1. Product exact SKU and safety/label requirements.
2. Supplier legal identity and fulfilment capacity verified.
3. Actual quoted costs, not generic listing prices.
4. Local Nigeria price benchmark.
5. Client consent and owner review.
6. Founder approval before any named outreach, purchase, contract or deployment.
No speculative China trip until verified benefit exceeds full travel cost and remote alternatives.
