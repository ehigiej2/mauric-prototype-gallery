# GATE 127 — Aduwawa × Northern Producers × Mining Services
Research-only integration. CROWN HOLD. No counterparties verified, contacted or onboarded.

## Verified geographic anchor
Published University of Benin research identifies Aduwawa cattle market as a Benin City livestock trading centre receiving animals from northern states. This is historical evidence, not current market volumes or prices.
Source: https://ubjst.uniben.edu/sites/default/files/2025-08/67-84.pdf

## Four linked trade journeys
1. Northern cattle breeder -> consented aggregator -> lawful veterinary and transport checks -> Aduwawa cattle market/abattoir -> butcher -> suya seller -> customer. Track provenance, condition, transport, food safety, quantity, verified payment; no speculative livestock prices.
2. Northern crop producer -> aggregation/quality grading -> authorised logistics -> Benin food seller/shop. Track crop grade, weight, spoilage, origin and verified acceptance.
3. Feed and agricultural input supplier -> farmer/breeder -> buyer; trace batch, input cost, delivery, approved financing only where legally authorised.
4. Licensed mining operator/registered buying centre -> permitted mineral supply chain and separately non-regulated procurement/logistics support. Mineral trading OFF by default until exact licence, provenance, title, lawful possession, environmental/labour and anti-illicit-finance checks are verified.

## Osayi voice workflows (no personal smartphone)
- Breeder: 'How many cattle have confirmed buyer offers? What is the transport cost?' Answer from consented verified records, UNKNOWN otherwise.
- Farmer: 'What produce can I sell and which route makes sense?' Compare offers, weights, costs and spoilage; user controls price.
- Suya trader: 'How much meat was received and what is my margin?' Use seller-confirmed inputs, not fabricated yield.
- Mining support provider: 'Help me quote lawful equipment repair or transport.' Human-approved quotation only; no extraction or mineral sale without legal gates.
- Local market woman/handyman: same shared voice gateway, separate tenant records.

## Quantitative routing
For each proposed trade route: landed_cost = purchase_price + transport + loading + veterinary/inspection + cold_chain + spoilage + taxes/fees; all variables sourced or UNKNOWN.
Gross contribution = verified selling price - landed_cost; no double charging.
For cattle, yield estimates require weighed verified slaughter data; no assumption of liveweight-to-meat conversion.
For crops, delivered_usable_kg = dispatched_kg - documented spoilage_kg.
For minerals, no sale price optimisation until permit/provenance and compliant buyer are verified.

## Mandatory controls
- Interstate animal movement: veterinary movement/loading permit and inspection as applicable, animal welfare, disease checks, official guidance. Source https://naqs.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/NAQS-Act-2018-1.pdf
- Mining: verify titles with Mining Cadastre Office, licensed mineral possession/purchase and buying-centre registration. Sources https://www.miningcadastre.gov.ng/ and https://portal.minesandsteel.gov.ng/Home/Guidelines
- Environmental, labour, safety and community protections; no illicit mining, child labour or conflict-linked sourcing.
- Privacy, consent, per-tenant isolation, existing banks, payment verification, free eligible solo-trader basic access, no autonomous deals, CROWN HOLD.
