## Commercial prerequisites
1. Client product brief: SKU/specification, quantity, acceptable substitutes, safety/certifications, destination, deadline, total landed-cost ceiling, buyer/importer of record, owner approval.
2. Client mandate: permission to seek quotes and negotiate; explicitly no purchase, deposit, signature, payment, personal-data sharing or binding promise without separate approval.
3. Supplier shortlist: legal registration and factory identity, beneficial ownership where necessary, production evidence, references, capacity, inspection access, bank beneficiary independently checked.
4. Freight shortlist: Midddleman freight-only acceptance UNKNOWN; compare independent warehouse/consolidation/freight providers, incoterms, customs broker and last mile.
5. Regulatory: Nigeria HS classification, SON/NAFDAC and other sector requirements as applicable, duties/VAT, customs broker/importer responsibility, China export compliance, sanctions/AML, anti-bribery, data protection.
6. Commercial protection: nonexclusive 6-12 month proposed framework subject to negotiation, quote validity, volume pricing, samples, acceptance criteria, defect remedy, IP, governing law/dispute venue, insurance, delivery evidence, termination, confidentiality, no hidden rebates.
7. Payment: authorised buyer pays verified supplier via protected platform or existing licensed bank. MAURIC never holds client funds without separately approved lawful arrangement.
8. Fees: optional fixed sourcing fee or percentage of independently verified net savings, disclosed and client-approved before engagement; zero double charge.

## Three comparable RFQ requests
**Supplier RFQ**: exact specification, MOQ and volume tiers (100/500/1000), EXW and FOB price, production lead time, packing dimensions/weight, sample terms, warranty, export eligibility, inspection and payment terms.
**China warehouse/freight RFQ**: will you receive independently sourced goods? Address, receiving/storage/handling, inspection, consolidation, chargeable weight, sea/air rate, insurance, duties and VAT included/excluded, clearance, Benin City last mile, liability and SLA.
**Independent inspection RFQ**: sampling plan, AQL or product-specific standard, factory audit scope, photo/video report, cost, turnaround, conflict independence.

## Like-for-like award model
For each compliant bid: total landed cost = goods + China inland + inspection + agent/warehouse + freight + insurance + customs duty + VAT + clearance + Nigeria delivery + FX/banking + documented expected loss. Exclude UNKNOWN quotes from price ranking. Check quality, capacity, supplier legitimacy, lead time and compliance before price comparison. Require two or more qualified alternatives; never invent savings.
