## Strategic position
**MAURIC × MONARCH × Waka × Osayi is an independent operating and intelligence layer for African everyday trade, not another captive marketplace, bank or delivery fleet.** It connects consented existing producers, traders, banks, transporters and independent service providers. MAURIC intelligence stays permissioned; participants own their commercial decisions and customer relationships. Do not assert live integration or actual outcomes without evidence.

## Beachhead and expansion
1. **Aduwawa ↔ northern cattle producers ↔ Benin butchers/suya sellers**: verified supply, lawful livestock movement and welfare, quality, weight/yield, transport quote, reconciliation, traceable existing-bank payment.
2. **Northern crop farmers ↔ Benin market women/shops**: quality grading, spoilage, route consolidation, verified delivered quantity, seller-controlled price.
3. **Trade support**: packaging, refrigeration, vehicle repairs, equipment sourcing, authorised logistics, inventory and business voice guidance.
4. **Mining-support services** only with legal compliance and separation from mineral ownership/trading. No mineral sale, title transfer or payments until licensing/provenance/AML and relevant environmental and worker safeguards independently verified.

## First customer and wedge
The first economic buyer is an existing trader, processor, aggregator or logistics coordinator who can measure reduced waste, lower empty miles, fewer failed deliveries, fewer stockouts or faster reconciliation. A smartphone-free trader accesses a shared, consented voice point where one is technically and legally feasible; not assumed to exist. Consumer acquisition comes later.

## Commercial design
- **Free**: verified eligible solo-trader basic tools (quote, stock, owner confirmation, accessible guidance), excluding real-world goods, logistics, taxes and third-party costs.
- **Paid only after value evidence**: optional verified service-booking fee, optional logistics coordination margin, multi-site seller operations subscription, B2B analytics and procurement savings share where independently measured and contracted.
- No default double-charging: every fee must have one named payer, one service and one event ID; never charge on a failed, cancelled or duplicate fulfilment.
- Payments via participant's existing licensed bank or provider; no holding client funds or unlicensed escrow/credit.
- Transparent opt-in price; no fees hidden in supplier quotes.

## Unit economics (illustrative scenario, NOT market prices)
Example pooled delivery: five individual dispatches at ₦1,800 each = ₦9,000 baseline; consolidated dispatch ₦6,300; total gross route saving ₦2,700. If platform charges a transparent ₦750 coordination fee in total, customer net savings = ₦1,950 before any changed handling or quality costs. The ₦750 is revenue, not profit; deduct actual platform servicing cost. Courier compensation and costs must be separately reconciled. This is a hypothesis, not an observed result.
