## RFQ template — UNSENT
Subject: Non-binding quotation — ready-stock, individual-order fulfilment to Nigeria
We are evaluating independent suppliers for an early-stage Nigeria market study. Please provide:
1. Registered legal company name, production vs trading role, factory/warehouse location and business licence;
2. Exact SKU, composition/material, colour, size chart, production and quality standard, photos of actual goods;
3. Ready stock per SKU and live inventory update method;
4. Price and MOQ for 1, 10, 50 units, EXW/FOB basis, currency, quotation expiry;
5. Whether single orders can be picked, packed and delivered to our nominated independent China warehouse, and per-order charges;
6. Dimensions, gross weight, domestic China freight, lead times, returns and defective-goods policy;
7. Sample policy, independent inspection rights, proof of past export experience;
8. Alibaba Trade Assurance eligibility and applicable order/payment terms.
This is a request for information only, not a volume commitment or authority to transact.
