## Acceptance: capability demonstrated, not attendance
Named trainees must independently, on exact production configuration and authorised test data:
- provision a clean device and enrol only the authorised test trader;
- diagnose a failed alert/network issue and safely restore service;
- reconcile queued requests without duplicate acceptance/fulfilment or false payment confirmation;
- migrate to a replacement device with authorised recovery and old access revoked;
- perform one agreed permitted maintenance task or correctly identify/escalate a prohibited repair;
- train another nominated operator from supplied documentation using teach-back;
- open and track a real supplier support test case only after separate contact authority.
Record app/firmware/model, task result, errors, reviewer and redacted evidence. Independent reviewer signs each agreed competence outcome; UNKNOWN/failed cases block training acceptance. Rework/retest remedy and independently assessed sign-off are to be agreed.
Negotiate a defined milestone/retention linked to accepted transfer deliverables; no amount or supplier agreement assumed. Hardware inspection and knowledge-transfer acceptance are separate obligations, both prerequisites for the relevant approval/release.
