# Revised core offer — ready-to-use trader order receiver
11 November 2026 | Concept and local simulation only | CROWN HOLD
Founder clarified packaging means complete trader kit, then proposed giving traders a device to receive orders when they do not want to download MONARCH. This document supersedes the phone-reuse default for that specific willing-trader segment, not every trader. Clothing remains paused.

## Offer
A dedicated supported Android phone/small screen supplied ready to use, in a stable counter stand, with appropriate charger/power solution, agreed connectivity and accessible handover. Trader opens shop, starts the shift and sees incoming order requests. Large controls: Accept, Decline, Help; prep time on acceptance where applicable. Minimal order information and trader-controlled availability. Existing personal phone, WhatsApp, bank, customers, suppliers and brand remain intact.
The trader does not download or configure an app on their personal phone. Authorised setup still provisions software/web runtime on the supplied device. This is not software-free hardware, and a web bookmark alone is not evidence of unattended receiving reliability.

## Complete cheap kit — proposed, no price quote
1. Supported phone-sized receiver selected for battery, loud/visible alerts, readable screen, repair availability and exact app/network tests. No new custom electronics/enclosure tooling or built-in printer by default.
2. Stable stock stand and safe correct charger/cable; battery runtime measured under workload. Add certified backup only if actual need; compatible input/output must be checked.
3. Data/SIM or agreed existing WiFi, disclosed recurring cost, signal check and backup contact consent. No assumption of dual-SIM failover or free data.
4. Existing protective OEM inserts/box retained where available and serviceable; appropriate plain shipping outer only when needed. One inexpensive band/label provides consistent identity without bulk custom cartons.
5. Folded counter/start card: trader/shop identity most prominent;3 pictorial steps, support route and warranty/loan terms. Optional shop-order QR only after an approved HTTPS destination exists, with visible matching URL; no live QR in this concept and no encoded login/customer/payment secrets.
6. Setup, shift-start alert test, brief teach-back, fault reporting, replacement route and ownership/exit terms. No unbudgeted gifting promise.
A phone owner who does not want a supplied device can remain on an agreed browser/WhatsApp/assisted route. Dedicated receiver is optional, not a condition of eligible free basic access.

## Reliable receiving architecture — requirements, not deployed
Authoritative server stores tenant-scoped order ID/version and expiry, queues delivery, records device receipt separately from trader acceptance, and never marks paid from a receipt/notification. Device opens only its enrolled tenant; enrolment credentials are never printed on public packaging.
Online transport can be websocket/polling plus platform-appropriate notification mechanisms. Reconnection reconciles from authoritative version/cursor. Android managed kiosk or OEM deployment route must be supported and lawfully sourced through appropriate EMM/OEM arrangements; do not assume general eligibility to Google's management APIs. Consent and exact management powers disclosed; no private-phone surveillance.
Push notification delivery, display wake, foreground/background audio and reboot recovery must be tested on exact device/OS/runtime. Permission/autoplay rules mean opening a web page is not enough. Startup includes a deliberate sound/visibility test and availability check. No blanket disabling of device security.
If offline: keep request at server, show receiver unavailable, do not claim new order delivered. A local accept made during disconnect is a pending intent, not confirmed fulfilment. Reconcile latest version, expiry, cancellation and availability before confirming. Duplicate deliveries must not duplicate acceptance, stock movement, charge or fulfilment.
If no acknowledgement: escalate after agreed configurable interval using separately authorised channels/contact and budget; record attempts/outcomes, not a guaranteed response. The local demo represents escalation as NOT SENT. Customer sees awaiting trader confirmation and an honest expiry; do not silently reroute to another seller without customer consent. Fresh food/stock-sensitive categories need short appropriate validity and stock recheck.

Per-trader economics must include receiver, stand, power, packaging, assembly, provisioning, data, support, replacements, repair freight, loss reserve and exit; printing only for a proven need. A shared printer requires consent, tenant/privacy-safe receipt handling and practical support/queues; not presumed cheaper.
Unknown acquisition/support/order margin means hardware subsidy viability UNKNOWN. Breakeven incremental completed orders per month = incremental monthly fully loaded cost / independently evidenced net incremental contribution per completed order, where contribution>0. No reliance on unverified volumes or promised savings. Hardware can be trader-owned, explicit funded gift or transparent loan only after founder/trader approval; no credit or contract presumed.

## Safety and acceptance
Preserve supplier packaging where adequate, avoid cable/charger movement or pressure on controls, retain warranty labels and serial records, protect against accidental activation and apply carrier-specific battery/transport requirements. Do not conceal required shipping/battery marks. UPS guidance: https://www.ups.com/gb/en/support/shipping-support/shipping-special-care-regulated-items/hazardous-materials-guide/how-to-ship-batteries . FedEx: https://www.fedex.com/en-gb/shipping/express.html . Folded card is not a load-bearing phone stand; no structural/durability claims.
Proposed gates: package movement/compression/moisture checks using test unit; stand stability and cable strain; normal operating heat; safe power; daylight/noisy-shop alert trial; reboot and overnight offline; device lost/reassigned; missed/duplicate/cancelled/expired orders; separate payment confirmation; data minimisation and trader accessibility. Formal shipping qualification requires relevant packaging/carrier test protocol; informal prototype checks are not certification.
