# Proposed trader hardware requirements
These are MAURIC acceptance targets, not measured device performance or agreed supplier SLAs.

## Minimum useful kit by workflow
A. Existing phone: supported patched browser/OS, secure screen lock, usable camera/mic/speaker where consented, sufficient battery/storage, connectivity chosen by trader; no mandatory dedicated phone if shared-use privacy can be protected and tested.
B. Receipt work: compatible58/80mm printer chosen by actual receipt requirements, locally available paper, readable naira/text/QR, documented SDK/transport, power and error/status reporting. Digital receipt alternative avoids forcing hardware purchases.
C. High SKU count: camera scanning first if practical; dedicated1D/2D scanner only if accuracy/speed improve. USB HID requires focus management, Enter suffix/keyboard layout, leading-zero preservation, lookup/unknown code handling and no auto-charge. No inventory decrement merely from a scan.
D. Integrated terminal: quote exact hardware model, Android/SUNMI OS build, GMS availability if needed, patch/end-of-support dates, scanner/printer variant, RAM/storage,4G bands/SIM, secure app distribution, documented offline SDK support, charger and peripheral power. Android14/4GB64GB may be a quotation preference for new units, not a claim all devices have it; accept cheaper alternatives only after evidence-based performance/security review.
E. Payments: provider-terminal device control and acquirer approval remain separate. NFC/EMV headline is not Nigerian acquiring permission. Do not sideload MONARCH on a banking terminal or collect PIN/PAN; existing bank remains usable. Any additional provider account choice is trader-controlled and explicit.

## Benin field targets
Proposed eight-hour representative shift, target>=20% battery remaining without unsafe charging, documented transaction/scan/print workload and screen/network settings. Record actual result; do not infer runtime from mAh. Test brief and prolonged connectivity loss; printer paper-out, power loss mid-print, scan duplicates, restarts and recovery. Measure queue behaviour without claiming offline bank authorisation. UPS/power bank suitability depends on actual voltage/current/connectors; no random USB battery for a mains printer.

## Ownership and privacy
Before allocation: serial/asset record, lawful title, recipient consent, owner/worker roles, documented support, warranty, repair/replacement and return/exit process. Loan terms must disclose responsibility and fair appeal, with no unapproved debt or punitive automatic clawback. Gift cannot be promised without funding. Device-management access must be purpose-limited, consented and separately authorised; no blanket worker surveillance, private phone access or remote wipe of personal content. Tenant/role separation, minimum data, lawful retention, encrypted recovery and authorised wipe need real tests. >=90% eligible private data on-device remains a target, not achieved evidence.

## RFI and commercial targets
Compare1/5/10/50 device prices without implied volume commitments, China-direct versus Nigerian authorised stock, exact incoterm/landed scope, warranty region and activation date, DOA/returns, spare battery/printer head, support SLA and loaner, repair freight, serial validation and protected payment. Nonexclusive; no minimum stock commitment, mandatory subscription, undisclosed rebate or mandatory branding. Demo/loan equipment is a request only, never assumed free. No first shipment before written funded mandate and owner review.

## Economics
Compare equal workloads and service horizons using the24-month cost template. Include existing-phone incremental data/power/support and integration work; do not treat reuse as cost-free. Imported price advantage must exceed installation, support, repair freight, downtime, compliance and FX risks. Compare China direct, authorised Nigeria purchase, rental/demo where genuinely offered and reuse. Do not base subsidy on hoped-for transactions or speculative procurement savings.
