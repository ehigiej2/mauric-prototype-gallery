## What makes OEM customisation worthwhile
Maximum incremental development/tooling justified by cost alone = verified quantity×verified same-scope landed saving minus extra compliance, integration, support and carrying costs. Example additional NGN10m work costs10000/unit at1000 but2000/unit at5000; must be earned back. Prefer existing OEM platform/unchanged supported electronics, configured software, branding/packaging and local service. Do not commission new PCB/mould merely to remove features unless full bid proves savings over its lifecycle.
Our proposed manufacturer target110000 vs143500 price anchor yields33500 gross device difference; no claim of like-for-like achieved saving. Firmware/runtime/support/lifecycle must first match, taxes and destination scope must reconcile, customisation must be validated. Knowledge-transfer scope is mandatory, priced and acceptance-linked.
