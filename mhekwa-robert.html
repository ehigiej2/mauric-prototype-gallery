4. What value should Mhekwa measure?

  • More completed orders: enquiries that become approved, fulfilled sales.
  • Better stock decisions: fewer oversells, stockouts and avoidable emergency purchases.
  • Delivery control: quoted versus actual delivery cost, successful first attempts and proof of delivery.
  • Faster administration: time spent from enquiry to quote, reconciliation and customer update.
  • Cash discipline: order value, supplier payment due, customer payment received and outstanding balance.

These are measurable value levers, not claimed savings. Baseline and outcome data must be gathered with Robert's permission before reporting realised value.
